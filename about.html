Orbit Beyond Private Limited

An Indian company, built to design and fly lunar systems.

The engineering and manufacturing arm of the ORBITBeyond group: incorporated in India on 27 December 2022 and recognised by DPIIT as a startup in Space Technology.

Who we are

Two entities. One mission.

The ORBITBeyond group operates as a two-entity structure. A US parent delivers lunar missions for NASA as one of only ten providers certified under the Commercial Lunar Payload Services programme. Orbit Beyond Private Limited is the India company that designs, integrates and manufactures the hardware those missions fly.

Our product scope covers the OB-1 lunar lander, surface rovers, small satellites, lunar 5G communications systems and AI data-centre payloads for lunar-surface compute.

The programme draws on three of the very few engineering communities anywhere that have attempted a lunar soft landing: India's ISRO Chandrayaan team, NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, and Israel's Beresheet guidance, navigation and control engineers.

Why now

The market has moved to repeat-order cadence.

NASA's Ignite policy (2026) mandates a higher commercial-mission cadence. The lunar market has shifted from single missions to production-line economics, and reliability is the scarcest commodity in it.

90+

Lunar missions planned globally

Across 2026 to 2035, demand is for repeatable hardware, not one-off spacecraft.

1 of 10

NASA CLPS-certified providers

The group is one of ten certified lunar delivery providers worldwide.

3 of 7

Recent landings failed or tipped

Reliability wins. Landing gear and GNC heritage are the differentiator, and both sit at the heart of the India programme.

Engineering lineage

Three communities that have attempted a soft landing.

ISRO
Chandrayaan heritage

Former ISRO Chairman Dr. S. Somanath advises the programme. Chief Systems Engineer Dr. M. Krishnaswamy led Cartosat and guided Chandrayaan-1.

NASA
Marshall Space Flight Center

Mission control is paired with NASA Marshall in Huntsville, giving dual control during descent with no single point of failure.

Beresheet
SpaceIL GNC engineers

Guidance, navigation and control know-how from Israel's private lunar programme is co-developed with the India team.
