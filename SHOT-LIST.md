This is the camera path the site flies today, transcribed shot by shot. Generate each shot as its
own clip, in order, and stitch them into ONE MP4 (hard cuts or a 6-frame dissolve between shots are
both fine). Total about 60 seconds at 24 fps. Same ground throughout: the lunar south pole beside
Shackleton crater, whose high rim is on the horizon in the distance.

Paste the STYLE BLOCK from PROMPTS.md at the end of every prompt. For the seven machine shots,
start the generation from the matching still (PROMPTS.md, step 1) so the hardware stays stable.

The rules the page depends on:
- Camera completely still for the last 2 seconds of every machine shot (shots 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15).
- Every machine shot ends with the machine a little left of centre, filling about a third of the
  frame height, sunlit side toward the camera, sun upper left.
- Travel shots keep moving the whole time; they never stop.
