04 · Services

What we deliver, end to end.

From a payload slot on OB-1 to a serial production line in Odisha.

S01 · Today

Contract engineering

Full-system lander, orbiter and payload engineering on milestones: SRR, PDR, CDR, structures, integration and test. Anchor customer Orbit Beyond, Inc.; next, other lander and infrastructure primes.

S02 · Next

Own-IP products

Rover mobility platforms and Helium-3 extraction systems on India-owned IP, sold per unit with integration and mission support.

S03

Lunar power-as-a-service

VSAT vertical solar arrays with RHU and battery night survival, delivered as recurring power on the surface.

S04

Relay communications

Communications relay orbiter and lunar 5G co-development with Tejas Networks for landers, rovers and surface payloads.

S05

Lunar compute

LunarEdge AI data-centre prototype for inference, autonomy and sensor processing at the Moon.

S06

Mission support & manufacturing

Mission support services, plus rover, satellite and lander-component production with CTTC and Odisha's industrial base.

The first to host lunar systems manufacturing will hold it.

We are seeking engagement with the Government of Odisha, CTTC, IDCO and strategic industry partners.
