The facility

20 acres. Three components. One campus.

  • R&D Centre. Systems engineering, guidance-navigation-control, structures and thermal design: the design authority of the programme.
  • AI&T Facility. Assembly, integration and test in-house: clean rooms, thermal-vacuum, vibration and the lander integration hall.
  • Production Lines. Rover and satellite manufacturing scaling with demand, plus the AI data-centre line and Bhubaneswar Mission Control on 4 dedicated acres.
